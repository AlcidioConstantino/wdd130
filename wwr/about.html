Zambezi Rafting Adventures

Our mission is to provide safe, thrilling, and unforgettable outdoor adventures for individuals, families, and teams. We blend expert guidance, breathtaking scenery, and well-planned trips that create memories you will keep for a lifetime.

History

Founded in 2010, our company started with just two rafts and a passion for the river. Over the last decade, we have expanded to guide thousands of thrill-seekers down some of the most beautiful and exciting rapids in the country.

Today, we remain family-owned and driven by the same spirit of adventure. Our certified guides know every bend of the river and are committed to making each trip safe, memorable, and full of discovery.
